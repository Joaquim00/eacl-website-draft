Security alert

We have received reports of fraudulent emails impersonating members of the Association’s Board. The EACL Board only communicates using chineselinguistics.europe@gmail.com. The Association never asks individuals to advance personal funds or make bank transfers beyond the annual membership fee paid through the association’s PayPal account. Do not respond or pay.

Training and exchange

The EACL Spring Schools in Chinese Linguistics

The Spring Schools give MA and PhD students affiliated with European universities the opportunity to explore areas of Chinese linguistics not covered at their home institutions and to learn about expertise across Europe.

Previous schools: Leiden (2006), München (2007), Amsterdam (2008), Moscow (2009), Bochum (2010), Paris (2011), Berlin (2012), Prague (2013), Rome (2014), Amiens (2016), Naples (2019), Olomouc (2025), and Stuttgart (2026).

Books and research

Chinese Linguistics in Europe Series

The series publishes refereed selections of papers presented at the Association’s biannual conferences. Published volumes include Chinese Linguistics in Budapest (2006) and Chinese Linguistics in Leipzig (2008). Members receive a discount on books.

For EACL members

Submit news or an event

Submit a conference, seminar, workshop, initiative, or other announcement relevant to Chinese linguistics. An administrator or authorised Board member will review it before publication.

This demonstration shows the intended review workflow. Submissions are not stored or sent to moderators.

What happens next?

  1. Your submission is saved as Pending Review.
  2. The administrator and authorised moderators receive a notification.
  3. A moderator can edit, approve, or reject it.
  4. Approved items become standard website posts and can be reused in news pages and newsletters.

The submitter should be able to see the status of their own submissions.
